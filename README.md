### News, Scores & Reference

* [FIFA](https://www.fifa.com/) - The official governing body website with tournament coverage, rankings, rules, and global football news.
* [UEFA](https://www.uefa.com/) - Official source for UEFA competitions, fixtures, results, standings, and European football news.
* [ESPN Soccer](https://www.espn.com/soccer/) - Comprehensive coverage featuring live scores, news, analysis, transfers, and match reports from leagues worldwide.
* [Sofascore](https://www.sofascore.com/) - Live scores, detailed match statistics, player ratings, and fixture tracking across hundreds of competitions.
* [Transfermarkt](https://www.transfermarkt.com/) - Extensive database of player transfers, market values, contracts, squads, and historical records.

### Analytics & Learning

* [FBref](https://fbref.com/) - Rich statistical profiles for players, teams, leagues, and seasons with advanced performance metrics.
* [Understat](https://understat.com/) - Advanced expected goals (xG) and shot data for analyzing team and player performance.
* [StatsBomb](https://statsbomb.com/) - High-quality football analytics resources, research articles, and free datasets for deeper analysis.
* [The Coaches' Voice](https://www.coachesvoice.com/) - Tactical breakdowns and coaching insights explained by elite managers and professional players.
* [The Athletic Football](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/football/) - In-depth football journalism, tactical analysis, and feature stories from experienced reporters.
